How qualification works
  1. The top seven players in the race qualify directly.
  2. If a current-year Grand Slam champion is ranked 8–20, the eighth seat goes to the highest-ranked such champion (at most two are eligible; the second becomes the first alternate).
  3. Otherwise the eighth seat goes to the player ranked 8, and the next player is the first alternate.
